Renovating a kitchen in Hobart

Hobart's older housing makes kitchen renovations interesting. Victorian and Federation homes in New Town and West Hobart often have small, closed kitchens at the back of the house, and opening them up can mean dealing with structural walls and heritage considerations. Post-war homes in Lenah Valley and the Eastern Shore usually have simpler layouts to work with.

Do I need council approval for a new kitchen?

Usually not if the layout and structure stay the same, but removing walls or other structural work can need approval, and plumbing and electrical work must be done by licensed trades. See our renovation process guide.

What licence should the renovator have?

In Tasmania, residential building work of $20,000 or more needs a licensed builder and a written contract under the Residential Building Work Contracts and Dispute Resolution Act 2016.
