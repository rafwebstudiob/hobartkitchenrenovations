Renovating a kitchen in Bellerive

Bellerive is on the Eastern Shore, with older homes near the village and the waterfront.

Older homes in Bellerive often have small, closed kitchens at the back of the house. Opening them up can mean structural work, and old wiring or asbestos-containing materials may need attention.

Close to the water, salt air can be hard on hardware and appliances, so ask about suitable materials.
